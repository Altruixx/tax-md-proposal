      How the app works

      There are two sides to Tax MD: what your clients do, and what your team does. Clients only do a few easy things. Your team handles the hard part, and the app does a lot of the work automatically. Watch the yellow tag move between them, or tap any number to jump to a step.

      The client's steps

      1. Signs up on the website or the phone app, using an email code instead of a password.
      2. Gets approved by your team and picks a plan.
      3. Takes a photo of a receipt and uploads it. Receipts are most of what clients send; come in a few times a year.
      4. Gets reminders before every deadline.
      5. Checks the dashboard to see income, tax withheld and missing documents.
      6. Taps "Approve" when the tax return is ready.
      7. Downloads the proof once your team has filed.

      Your team's steps

      1. Approves new clients and assigns them to a staff member.
      2. Reviews receipts. The app reads each receipt and fills in the date, store, amount and category; staff check them.
      3. Matches 2307s with invoices. The app flags anything missing.
      4. Exports to Excel if needed, and prepares the tax return.
      5. Sends it for approval to the client in the app.
      6. Files with the BIR and uploads the proof for the client.

      Costs after launch

      These are not part of the ₱750,000. Once Tax MD is live, it needs a database, servers and an AI service to keep running. These costs grow slowly as you get more clients, and they stay small next to what each client pays. Pick a number of clients:

      What we assumed Receipts, file sizes, AI cost
      • Each client uploads about 30 receipts and 2 other documents (like 2307s) a month.
      • The app shrinks each photo to about 0.4 MB, so each client adds about 150 MB of files a year.
      • Reading one receipt with a low-cost AI model costs about ₱0.20.
      • The domain taxmd.ph costs US$42.36 a year on Porkbun (about ₱2,630), shown split per month.
      • Numbers are monthly, after one full year of receipts is stored. Pesos use about ₱62 per US dollar.
      • The database plan includes 100 GB of file storage, enough for about 650 clients' receipts for a full year. After that, extra storage costs very little. At 500 and 1,000 clients, the database needs a stronger server, which is why that cost goes up.
